FAQ

Common questions

Don't see your question? Call 585-806-6064 and we'll be happy to help.

How much does a cleaning cost?

We don't use a fixed price list. Every home is different, so we give each customer a free estimate based on the size of the home and the type of cleaning needed.

How do I get a free estimate?

Fill out the estimate form on this page, call us at 585-806-6064, or email bellacoastcleaning@yahoo.com. Tell us about your home and the kind of cleaning you need.

Do you offer a discount for new customers?

Yes. New customers may receive a special first-time cleaning discount. Ask about it when you request your estimate.

What's the difference between regular and deep cleaning?

Regular cleaning keeps your home fresh week to week: dusting, vacuuming, mopping, kitchen and bathroom surfaces. Deep cleaning goes further into built-up grime, baseboards, fixtures and hard-to-reach areas. It's a great first visit or seasonal refresh.

Can I set up recurring cleanings?

Yes. We offer recurring cleaning on a schedule that works for you, such as weekly, bi-weekly or monthly.

Do you clean before or after a move?

Yes. We offer both move-in and move-out cleaning so your new home is ready and your old one is left spotless.

Can I customize what gets cleaned?

Absolutely. Let us know your priorities, like the kitchen, bathrooms or specific rooms, and we'll tailor the visit to your needs.

What areas do you serve?

Palm Coast, Florida and the surrounding areas. If you're nearby and not sure we cover your address, just ask.
